## Bunny Races

Bet on a first/second-place pair (tickets 1000 Fol each, up to 8 at once) among bunnies with
Speed/Stamina/Personality stats. Longer-shot wins pay better prizes. Roughly best-to-worst prize
order: Bunny Shoes, Magical Drops, Ganza Sea Urchin, Seltzer, Wisdom Ring, Lunatic Ring, Fairy Ring,
Cinderella Glass, Fairy Glass, Reverse Doll, Dummy Doll, 50,000 Fol, Protection Bomb, Tetra-bomb,
Stone Check, Paralysis Check, Poison Check, Spring Water, Resurrection Bottle, Blackberry,
Aquaberry, Blueberry.

## Cooking Master

A timed (5-minute) minigame: combine ingredients from a central pool into dishes for points; a
"Pressure gauge" (eased by higher Courage skill) makes success harder as it rises. Score = sum of
each dish's HP/MP recovery %; beat a random opponent (usually ~1000 points) to win that match's
ingredient set as a prize:

| Battle | Prize |
|---|---|
| Meat | 10 Meat, 10 Egg/Dairy Products, 3 Creamy Cheese, 3 Juicy Beef |
| Seafood | 20 Seafood, 1 Ganza Sea Urchin, 2 Prime Tuna |
| Veggie | 10 Vegetables, 10 Grain, 1 Magical Rice, 2 Purity Leaf |
| Slime | 10 Slippery Slime, 3 Jiggly Slime |
| Dessert | 10 Fruit, 4 Sweet Fruit |
| Full-course | 6 each Meat/Seafood/Vegetables/Fruit, 8 Egg/Dairy, 1 Juicy Beef, 2 Purity Leaf, 4 Sweet Fruit, 1 Ganza Sea Urchin, 3 Creamy Cheese, 3 Magical Rice, 3 Prime Tuna |

Beating all chefs plus a secret opponent ("Puffy") with one character unlocks a final bout against
"Yarma" — winning retires that character from the contest but awards a unique "Master of EATS" item.

## Battle Arena — four modes, each with 5 ranks (F/E through A)

**Duel Battle** (1v1): prizes differ for fighters (Killer Moves) vs. spellcasters at ranks B-E;
Rank A gives a character-specific unique weapon (e.g. Claude → Windsley Sword, Dias → Cromlea Sword,
Rena → Fellper Nails — full per-character list available in the source if needed later).

**Team Battle** (five sequential 1v1s, win 3 of 5): rank prizes — F: Purple Mist, E: Zephyr Earring,
D: Magic Cross, C: Dream Crown, B: Right Cross, **A: Star Guard**.

**Bully Battle** (one character vs. a wave of enemies): rewards scale from 1,000 Fol/2 SP at Rank F
up to 80,000 Fol/100 SP at Rank A — also a fast way to grind SP.

**Survival Battle**: a 50-fight gauntlet against progressively harder enemies, recommended only at
character level ~110+. Reward for completion: the "Fortune" accessory (drops bonus items while
walking).
